## 1. Cost & scale of healthcare data fragmentation

- **$4,542** higher annual spend for patients in high-fragmentation care; **57.9%** of adults
  affected by high fragmentation (2025); **$30B/yr** recoverable via device interoperability;
  **$20M/yr** per provider in data-error costs
  ([blueBriX, 2025](https://bluebrix.health/blogs/connecting-the-dots-eliminating-fragmented-systems-workflows-for-coordinated-care)).
- **$200B/yr** in duplicate services ([Chief Healthcare Executive, 2025](https://www.chiefhealthcareexecutive.com/view/consequences-of-fragmented-healthcare-information-systems)).
- **$17–29B/yr** medical-error costs (IoM, cited 2025).
- McKinsey: **$1 trillion** wasted US health spend (20–25%), 50–75% eliminable via shared platform.
- Clinician burden: **4.5 hrs/day** in EHR ([Medical Economics, 2025](https://www.medicaleconomics.com/view/physicians-spend-4-5-hours-a-day-on-electronic-health-records));
  3:1 EHR-to-patient ratio ([athenahealth](https://www.athenahealth.com/resources/blog/insights-from-2025-pss));
  1.2–1.3 hrs/day "pajama time"; documentation = #1 burnout cause
  ([Tebra](https://www.tebra.com/theintake/ehr-emr/how-documentation-became-top-cause-of-physician-burnout)).

## 3. "Why now" — regulatory drivers (global)

- **CMS-0057-F** (Interoperability & Prior Auth): FHIR APIs required; 72-hr expedited / 7-day
  standard PA decisions; up to $1M/violation for info blocking
  ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-finalizes-rule-expand-access-health-information-and-improve-prior-authorization-process)).
- **ONC HTI-1/HTI-2:** USCDI v3 mandatory Jan 1 2026; USCDI v6 (Jul 2025), Draft v7 (Jan 2026);
  TEFCA (HTI-2 effective Jan 15 2025), ~500M records exchanged
  ([ONC](https://isp.healthit.gov/united-states-core-data-interoperability-uscdi);
  [HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/tefca-americas-national-interoperability-network-reaches-nearly-500-million-health-records-exchanged.html)).
- **EU EHDS** (Reg 2025/327): in force May 26 2025; provider certification Jan 2026; key provisions
  by Mar 2029 ([EC](https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space-regulation-ehds_en)).

  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/28/billion-dollar-infrastructure-deals-ai-boom-data-centers-openai-oracle-nvidia-microsoft-google-meta/)).
- "Nascent health AI data infrastructure category will blossom" — Bessemer State of Health AI 2026
  ([BVP](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/state-of-health-ai-2026)).
- RAG reduces hallucinations 70–90% ([iatrox](https://www.iatrox.com/blog/rag-in-healthcare-benefits-evidence-safe-deployment-guide)).
